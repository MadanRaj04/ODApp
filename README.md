# On-Duty: Pro

**On-Duty: Pro** is a mobile application designed to simplify the process of managing, creating, viewing, and validating College On-Duty (OD) requests. Tailored for students and faculty, this app helps users efficiently track attendance and manage leave requests with ease.

## Features

### 1. **Create OD Requests**
Easily submit your OD requests through the app. Provide essential details such as:
- Reason for leave
- Date of the leave
- Any supporting documentation (if applicable)

### 2. **View OD History**
Keep a comprehensive record of all your OD requests and their statuses. Access your complete OD history to monitor your attendance and leave patterns.

### 3. **Faculty Dashboard**
Faculty members can:
- Review, validate, and manage OD requests from students.
- Streamline the approval process and provide timely responses to leave requests.

### 4. **Secure and Confidential**
Your data is important to us. All your OD-related information is securely stored, and access is restricted to authorized users only.

### 5. **User-Friendly Interface**
Enjoy a simple and intuitive interface that makes navigating the app effortless. Submitting OD requests and checking their status is just a few taps away.

## Requirements

- A stable internet connection for optimal app performance.
- Designed for college students and faculty members.

## Usage

1. **Students** can use the app to create and manage their OD requests, view their OD history, and track the status of their requests.
2. **Faculty members** can use the app to review and validate OD requests from students, ensuring a streamlined approval process.
